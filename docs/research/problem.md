## 4. Officially stated and widely reported rejection reasons

- The 2024-25 coverage names five reasons:
  - name mismatch across Aadhaar, PAN and EPFO
  - DOB mismatch
  - unverified bank account
  - multiple UANs
  - previous employer not updating the exit date

  Source: [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/news/story/epfos-instant-pf-withdrawal-promise-has-a-catch-one-in-five-claims-still-gets-rejected-541466-2026-07-07), 2026-07-07.
- A former CBT member says many rejections now come from "just an alphabet in the name of the member not matching, or different details in Aadhaar". Source: [Deccan Herald / Indian Express](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fone-in-three-epf-final-settlement-claims-rejected-report-2908705).
- **Real remarks look different.** In 148 real remarks (`ml/decoder/data/public_remarks.jsonl`), 43% are EPS/pension membership, contribution/ECR gaps, para 26(6) higher-wage declarations, TDS/15G, or transfer-in pending. These are mostly employer-side data problems, not member KYC.

## Insights for the product

1. **The pain is large and has a measured size.** About 174 lakh claims were rejected in FY25 (about 22%), and a further 7.8% are returned. That makes "fix before filing" a real job, even though rejection rates are falling.
2. **Most rejections can be avoided.** The top official reasons (name, DOB, father's name, bank, exit date) are all checkable before filing. That supports the Pre-check rules as the core feature.
3. **The decoder's label set misses the biggest real bucket.** EPS membership, contribution/ECR gaps, para 26(6), TDS/15G and transfer-in pending make up 43% of real remarks. Add 3–5 categories for these before training more.
4. **Many fixes need the employer, not the member:** JD, revised ECR/3A, genuineness letter, exit reason. A consultant who also runs the employer's ECR is in exactly the right seat. Pitch the product as "employer-side fix steps".
5. **Auto-settlement is shrinking the easy cases.** 2.16 crore auto claims in 3 days, with the limit now Rs 5 lakh. The consultant's value moves to the messy cases: high-value, EPS, transfers. The product should focus there and not on simple Form 19s.
6. **Pricing at Rs 499–999/month is under the Rs 1,000–2,400/month consultants charge each client.** It is plausible only if the tool covers many clients per consultant. Per-claim member fees (Rs 5,000–10,000) show rework is costly, so "one avoided rejection pays for a year" is a fair pitch, but it is not proven.
7. **No competitor was found that decodes remarks or pre-checks claims for consultants.** Payroll SaaS stops at ECR and Form 19 generation. This is open space, but absence of evidence is weak, so check RazorpayX and Saral directly.
8. **Remarks are cryptic.** Examples include "ABC", "CONT RCVD UPTO05/17SUB DLY SUBSN CLRIFY", and system errors like "MISSING RIGHT PARENTLESIS". That is a strong demo story for the decoder, and also a warning that some remarks have no fix and only mean "resubmit".

## Caveats

- **The rates are not the same measure.** The overall rate (about 22%), the rejection-only rate (13.77%) and the final-settlement rate (about 34%) count different things, so do not compare them directly.
- **Grievance numbers are secondary.** Verify them on sansad.in (LS USQ 2680, 9 Mar 2026).
- **Fee data is thin.** It comes from marketplace listings and vendor blogs, not a survey, and there are no JustDial or Urban Company prices.
- **No average settlement days and no consultant-adoption numbers were found.**
