# Rule Book check against official sources

Checked on **2026-10-15** against public official sources: PIB / Ministry of Labour press releases, EPFO pages, and the gazette text of the 2026 schemes. Law-firm and news summaries were used only to find or back up an official decision. These are not legal advice. The consultant still confirms every rule, and confirmed rules (`last_verified_by` set) are never overwritten by re-seeding.

Note: epfindia.gov.in now redirects to epfo.gov.in, and many old PDF links return 404. PIB pages work.

## What changed recently

1. **New schemes in force from 1 July 2026.** The EPF Scheme, 2026 (G.S.R. 525(E), dated 29 Jun 2026), the EPS, 2026 and the EDLI Scheme, 2026 replaced the 1952, 1995 and 1976 schemes under the Code on Social Security, 2020. The CBT approved them on 2 Mar 2026.
2. **Final PF settlement now has a 12-month wait (it was 2 months)**: EPF Scheme 2026 para 49(2). The CBT decided this on 13 Oct 2025, and it is now notified and in force. On losing a job a member can still take 75% straight away as a partial withdrawal (PIB, 15 Oct 2025).
3. **Pension withdrawal (Form 10C) now has a 36-month wait (it was 2 months)**, counted from the date the last contribution was due, or until retirement age if that comes first (EPS 2026).
4. **Partial withdrawals were merged into 3 types**: essential needs, housing, and special circumstances. Every type needs 12 months of membership. A 25% minimum balance must stay in the account; the rest can be withdrawn, with a minimum of ₹1,000 per withdrawal (EPF Scheme 2026 para 46).
5. **Wage ceiling raised from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month, from 17 Sep 2026** (Union Cabinet, PIB 16 Sep 2026).
6. Already in force: auto-settlement of advances up to ₹5 lakh (Jun 2025); no cheque image needed for online claims, and no employer approval for bank seeding (Apr 2025); profile self-correction for Aadhaar-verified UANs (Jan 2025); transfer claims without employer routing (Jan 2025), auto-credited once the source office approves (Apr 2025); UAN creation by face authentication in UMANG (Apr 2025).
7. **TDS**: from tax year 2026-27 (Income Tax Act, 2025), Form 121 replaces Form 15G/15H for PF withdrawals.
8. **EPFO 3.0**: the CBT approved it in Oct 2025, and PIB (Feb 2026) describes it as an ongoing project. I found no official source saying ATM or UPI withdrawal is live, so nothing was added.

## Code changes that follow

- `checks.ts`: R-WAITING-PERIOD `days` can now be a number or a per-form map. The default is `{ '19': 366, '10C': 1096 }`. The fix texts for bank verification and exit date were updated.
- `seed.ts`: every rule has its own `source`. Re-seeding refreshes unverified rows and clears the embedding when the title or body changes.
